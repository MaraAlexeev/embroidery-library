BEFORE THE FIRST STITCH

A little preparation
goes a long way.

01

Choose your pattern

Each design includes a PES file, an editable source, a preview, and instructions. These designs fit a 100 × 100 mm hoop.

02

Read the thread order

Follow the numbered loading sequence. A color may appear more than once, and the machine's display palette may look different.

03

Try it on scrap

Check each design's testing notes. Fabric, stabilizer and machine setup affect the result. All images shown here are software previews.
